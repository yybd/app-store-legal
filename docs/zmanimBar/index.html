ZmanimBar — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 8, 2026

ZmanimBar is built privacy-first. The app calculates halachic times (zmanim) locally on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data, and the app works fully offline. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

Location

If you enable automatic location, ZmanimBar uses your device’s location solely on your device to compute accurate zmanim for where you are. Your coordinates are never sent to us and never leave your device — except that, to display your city name, the coordinates may be passed to Apple’s geocoding service, which is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy. You can instead set a city manually and not use location at all.

Calendar

If you choose to add a zman to your calendar, ZmanimBar requests permission to add events to Apple Calendar. It only writes the events you explicitly ask it to add. It does not read, upload, or share your calendar data.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device through Apple’s notification system. No reminder content is sent to any server.

Purchases

The optional one-time “Pro” upgrade is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information.

Data Storage

Your settings and any custom times you create are stored locally on your device (and shared on-device with the ZmanimBar widget). This information is not transmitted off your device.

Children

ZmanimBar is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
