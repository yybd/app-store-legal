RSync Pro

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 9, 2026

The short version: RSync Pro is built so that your data stays yours. We do not collect, transmit, or have access to any of your information. Everything the app stores — your sync configurations, SSH keys, and logs — stays on your Mac. When you run a sync, your files travel directly between your Mac and the destination you choose (a local folder, or your own server over an encrypted SSH connection). They never pass through us.

1. Who we are

RSync Pro (“the app”) is developed and published by Y. Ben David (BD-Tech). This policy explains how the app handles information. If you have any questions, contact us at support.orsync@bd-tech.com.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal data. The app contains:

  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No third-party tracking or marketing SDKs
  • No account, sign-in, or registration
  • No telemetry sent to the developer

We never see your files, folder names, server addresses, credentials, or how you use the app.

Crash diagnostics

If you have chosen to share diagnostics with app developers in macOS System Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, Apple may make anonymized, aggregated crash reports available to us through Apple’s systems. This is operated by Apple, is governed by Apple’s privacy policy, is opt-in, and contains no information that identifies you. We do not operate any analytics of our own.

3. Information the app stores on your device

To do its job, the app saves the following locally on your Mac, inside its sandboxed application container. This information never leaves your device except as you direct (see “Network connections” below):

  • Sync configurations — profile names, source and destination folder paths, and, for remote syncs, the server host name, user name, and port you enter.
  • Security-scoped bookmarks — macOS references that let the app reopen the specific folders you granted it access to, across launches.
  • SSH keys — key pairs the app generates, or existing keys you select, for connecting to your own servers.
  • App settings — your preferences, such as the idle-timeout option.
  • Logs — records of your sync runs, kept locally so you can review what happened.

You can delete any of this at any time from within the app, and removing the app deletes all of it.

4. Network connections

The app connects only to destinations you explicitly configure:

  • Your servers. When you run a remote sync or test a connection, the app connects to the server you specified, over SSH (an encrypted protocol). Your files and the credentials needed to authenticate are sent only to that server — never to us or to any third party.
  • Local network discovery (optional). If you use “Discover on network”, the app uses Bonjour to look for computers on your local network that offer Remote Login (SSH). This scan happens entirely on your Mac; the results are shown only to you and are not sent anywhere.

The app does not contact any server operated by the developer.

5. SSH keys and passwords

  • SSH private keys created or used by the app are stored locally in the app’s container and protected by file permissions.
  • If you authenticate with a password (for example, to install a key on a server), the password is used only to make that connection. It is held transiently in a file readable only by you, is never transmitted to the developer, and is not retained beyond the operation.

6. Third-party services

The app uses no third-party analytics, advertising, or cloud services. The file-transfer engine bundled with the app (openrsync) is open-source software that runs locally on your Mac; it is not a service and sends nothing to its authors or to us.

7. Children’s privacy

The app is a general-purpose utility, is not directed to children, and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

8. Data retention and deletion

Because all data is stored locally, you are in full control:

  • Delete individual sync profiles, schedules, history entries, or SSH keys from within the app.
  • Uninstalling RSync Pro removes the app and its locally stored data from your Mac.

9. Security

The app runs inside the macOS App Sandbox, which limits its access to your system. SSH connections are made in an isolated configuration, and credentials and keys are stored within the app’s protected container. While no software can guarantee absolute security, the app is built to keep your data on your device and under your control.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date shown at the top of this page. Material changes will be reflected here.

11. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your privacy? Email support.orsync@bd-tech.com and we’ll be glad to help.
